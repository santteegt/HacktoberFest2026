# Sticker tracker

| Stickers | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | Mailed Hacktoberfest 2026 sticker pack |
| 10 | Bonus holographic sticker in the pack |
| 15 | Completionist: raffle for a t-shirt or Arduino Uno Q |

The two **Required** stickers must be earned first. Signing in alone does not qualify you.
