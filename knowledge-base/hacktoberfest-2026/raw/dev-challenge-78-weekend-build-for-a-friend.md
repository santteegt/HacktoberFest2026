## Prompt

Build something with **open-source AI at its core**. That can mean running an open-weight model, building on an open-source agent harness or framework, running inference locally, or all three. The open pieces should be what makes the project work.

In the post, explain **why open matters** for what you built: does it run offline on a laptop, keep someone's data off a server they don't control, let you fine-tune or swap models or change agent behavior, cost nothing to run? Say where the open approach worked better than a closed one.

**Each submission must be a new project built during the challenge window.**

## Theme: Build for a Friend

Ship something that solves a real problem for a friend or someone you love. Pick one real person and build something for them. It doesn't have to be big, but it has to matter to them. Examples from the page:

- A meal planner that knows your roommate's allergies
- A patient practice partner for a friend learning a new language
- A tool that turns your grandpa's voice memos into a family recipe book

Bonus points if you actually hand it over and report what they said.

## Judging criteria

1. **Writing Quality** (weighted most heavily): clear, engaging; explains what you built, who it's for, why open matters
2. **Relevance to the Prompt and Theme**: is open-source AI at the core? does it fit "Build for a Friend"?
3. **Creativity**: original idea or fresh take
4. **Technical Execution**: does it work, is it well built
5. **Use of Partner Technology** (optional): meaningful use, if entering a prize category

Optional: save your agent session with DevRelay and embed/link it in the post to show your process.

## How to participate

Publish a DEV post using the **submission template on the announcement post**, with the required tag `#hf26challenge`. Tell what you built and who it's for, share a demo and your code, and explain why open matters. Teams: one member publishes and lists teammates' DEV usernames in the body (teams of up to four, per DEV rules).

## Related rules (from DEV Official Rules, see KB `dev-challenges`)
