# Global Hack Week (GHW)

**Last verified:** 2026-09-30 against `ghw.mlh.com` (home, `/swag`, `/faq`,
`/sponsor`, `/schedule`, `/events/open-source`), the live challenge dashboard
that `ghw.mlh.io/challenges` redirects to, the Hacktoberfest 2026 schedule feed
(`hacktoberfest-api.mlh.com/api/schedule`), the MLH Organizer Guide pages on
digital mini-events and OrganizerHQ, and the MLH Fellowship FAQ.

This is the general guide to how Global Hack Week works: registration,
challenges, points, swag, the schedule's shape, Discord, and Guilds. It
deliberately leaves out any one week's dates, theme, sessions, or challenge
list, which change every month.

> ⚠️ **For the current or next GHW, fetch live.** The event calendar is on
> `https://ghw.mlh.com`, the week's sessions are on
> `https://ghw.mlh.com/schedule`, and the week's challenges are at
> `https://ghw.mlh.io/challenges`, which redirects to the current event's
> dashboard. With DevRelay, `search_mlh_events` finds GHW events by name and
> date. Don't quote a GHW date or session from memory.

Related topics: `/knowledge/mlh-hackers` (MLH participation in general),
`/knowledge/mlh-policies` (Code of Conduct), `/knowledge/hacktoberfest`
(GHW: Hacktoberfest counts toward Hacktoberfest 2026 stickers).

## The one-paragraph version

Global Hack Week is a **free, online, week-long event that MLH runs every
month**. In MLH's words: *"Each month we host a Global Hack Week where you can
learn new skills, build your portfolio, attend fun sessions and connect with
other hackers in the MLH community."* It's *"a 100% free event for anyone,
anywhere!"* All you need is a computer. Each week has a theme. Recent and announced
themes include Agents, Data, a Hacktoberfest week on open-weight models,
beginner-friendly Builders Week, and Open Source Week. During the week, participants attend
livestreamed sessions, complete challenges for experience points, hang out in
Discord, build projects, and earn physical swag in the mail.

**Register:** `https://mlh.link/ghw` points at the current or next event. Each
week also has its own registration page on `events.mlh.com`.

## Who can join

From the FAQ, verbatim: *"All are welcome to attend and participate in Global
Hack Week no matter your experience level and student status. Hundreds of
hackers who have never coded before will attend GHW. This event is geared for
folks learning to code as well as experts! Minors 13 and over are welcome to
attend with a parent's permission."*

GHW follows the **MLH Code of Conduct** (see `/knowledge/mlh-policies`).

## Registration

A GHW registration form has asked for:

- Country of residence
- Age confirmation: 18+, or parental permission for minors
- Agreement to the MLH Code of Conduct
- Agreement to the MLH Contest Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy
- A GitHub username
- A commitment to fill out the shipping address form so swag can be sent
- *Optional:* student email, a GitHub Student Developer Pack opt-in, and partner
  communication preferences

GHW registrations run through MLH's registration system. MLH's organizer guide
shows an OrganizerHQ dashboard captioned *"An example from Global Hack Week
Signups."* If you already have an MLH account, you answer fewer questions.

## How a week works

- **Daily livestreamed workshops** on Twitch (`https://www.twitch.tv/mlh`), many
  of them partner-led
- **Challenges**, which are social, technical, design, and no-code tasks that
  earn experience points
- **Mini-events** in the MLH Community Discord
- **Building projects**, solo or with others
- **Opening and closing ceremonies** that bookend the week

### The schedule's shape

The live schedule (`https://ghw.mlh.com/schedule`) has **Day 1 through Day 6**
tabs. Each session card has a date, a start time **in ET**, a title, and a
Register link on `events.mlh.com`. A GHW week runs six programming days, with
the closing ceremony on the day after Day 6.

Recurring session types:

| Session | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Opening Ceremony** | Starts the week |
| **"Today in Global Hack Week — Day N"** | A daily stream about what's happening that day |
| **Technical workshops** | Often two-part (Part 1, Part 2) and often partner-led |
| **Mini-events** | Community games in Discord. The GHW: Hacktoberfest schedule lists Typeracer, Tetris, Gartic Phone, and Wiki Races |
| **Closing Ceremony** | Ends the week |

Session times vary week to week, so read them from the live schedule.
Recordings go to Twitch and YouTube (`https://www.youtube.com/@MajorLeagueHacking`).

> ⚠️ **The schedule page can lag.** On 2026-08-06 `/schedule` was still showing
> the previous week's lineup, and the day tabs render client-side. If the page
> looks stale, check the event on `events.mlh.com` and say so.

## Challenges and points

Challenges are the core mechanic. They earn **experience points** and are part
of qualifying for swag.

- **Where to submit:** *"Challenges are submitted to the dashboard at
  <https://ghw.mlh.io/challenges>."* That URL redirects to the current event's
  page at `https://www.mlh.com/events/<event-slug>/challenges`.
- **Dashboard filters:** Most Popular, Beginner, Partner, No Code, and
  Registration, plus a filter by partner.
- **Kinds of challenges:**
  - **Registration:** check in to the week and provide your address on your MLH
    profile. Some weeks include setup tasks like signing up for the GitHub
    Student Developer Pack.
  - **Partner:** hands-on tasks with a partner's product.
  - **Tooling:** GitHub, Codespaces, Copilot, and similar tools.
  - **No-code and creative:** tasks that need no programming.
  - **Social:** community and Discord participation.
- **Participant counts:** challenge cards show counts like 10+, 50+, 100+.
  These show popularity. **They are not point values.**
- **Challenge sets are rebuilt every week.** Fetch the dashboard for the current
  list.

**Point thresholds matter elsewhere.** Hacktoberfest 2026, for example, awarded
stickers for earning 15, 30, and 75 points at GHW: Hacktoberfest (see
`/knowledge/hacktoberfest`). MLH doesn't publish how many points each challenge
is worth.

## Swag

*"There is plenty to be excited about at Global Hack Week. We want to make sure
you have a chance to rep Global Hack Week long after the week ends."*

**Basic swag, a sticker pack and a postcard,** requires being *fully checked
in*. The swag page lists:

1. Joining the **MLH Community Discord** server
2. Submitting an address at **`mlh.link/Address`**
3. Completing **all registration challenges**

The FAQ's version also includes *"checking in on the event registration page."*

**Bonus swag, extra stickers,** requires attending **at least 5 live sessions**
during the week.

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Character Sticker** | *"Each Global Hack Week features a new character sticker to show off on your water bottle, laptop, or anywhere else you display your stickers."* |
| **Sticker Sheet** | *"Use our Global Hack Week sticker pack to deck out your laptop."* |
| **Postcard** | *"After each GHW you attend, you'll receive a postcard from us in the mail!"* |
| **Hex Sticker** | *"Build up an awesome pattern on your devices as you earn more and more of our hex stickers."* |

**Cost:** free, both taking part and swag.

### Shipping

Swag arrives as a **letter by post, shipped from the United States**: through
USPS inside the US, and through the destination country's postal system
elsewhere.

| Destination | After MLH sends | After the event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside the US | 1–2 weeks | Up to 4–6 weeks |
| Outside the US | 3–4 weeks | Up to 6–8 weeks |
| India | Highly variable by region | *"If it's been more than 12 weeks since the event, your letter may have been lost in the Indian postal system."* |

MLH **does ship to India**. Keep your address current at `mlh.link/Address`.

## Community: the MLH Discord

*"We use Discord to connect throughout the week. Make sure you've joined our
server to stay up to date with all things Global Hack Week."* Join at
**`https://discord.mlh.io`**. Mini-events and live sessions happen in Discord
alongside the Twitch streams. Joining is also part of basic-swag check-in.

## Guilds

Guilds are groups that participants organize within GHW, so you can bring your
own crowd into the week.

- **What to form one around (verbatim):** *"A guild can be formed around
  anything. Former guilds have been hosted around individual hackathons
  (shoutout to the Girls Hoo Hack guild), your timezone (shoutout to ESTechies),
  or your love of stuffed sharks from IKEA (shoutout to Blahajgang)!"*
- **Why organize (verbatim):** *"We know that there is a ton of imposter
  syndrome in our community. We are confident in each and every one of you and
  will make sure you have all the tools you need to shine as guild leader."*
- **Not feeling ready (verbatim):** *"Guilds are a fantastic way to market your
  hackathon, form a stronger community at your school, meet people with similar
  interests as you, or practice your leadership skills in a low-stakes
  environment."*

## Why GHW matters beyond the week

- **The MLH Fellowship requires it, or a hackathon.** Eligibility includes
  having *"been a participant in at least one MLH Hackathon or Global Hack Week
  event."* See `/knowledge/mlh-hackers`.
- **Partner programs.** GHW weeks can be themed with partners. Hacktoberfest
  2026 included a GHW week that counted toward its sticker book.

## Sponsorship

Page: `https://ghw.mlh.com/sponsor`. *"Global Hack Week offers authentic
partnerships with our developer community programs. We are experts at creating
sponsorship opportunities that position companies alongside early career
technologists in meaningful, repeatable, and long-lasting ways."* Companies
fill out the form on that page. **No tiers, pricing, benefits, or audience
numbers are published.** The partner logos on the site carry no alt text, so
don't guess at GHW's sponsor list. The partners behind a given week show up
through its partner-led workshops and challenges.

## History

**Local Hack Day became Global Hack Week in early 2022.** MLH's explanation,
verbatim: *"In early 2022, we decided to change Local Hack Day to Global Hack
Week. As Local Hack Day grew, it became a week-long event featuring technical
live streams, workshops, career chats, panels, games, and more. We wanted to
update our language to reflect how this event series has brought together
hackers from every corner of the globe for weeks at a time."*

GHW's sites moved from `ghw.mlh.io` to `ghw.mlh.com`. The `.io` links still
redirect.

## The GHW FAQ, verbatim

MLH's own answers from `https://ghw.mlh.com/faq`.

### General

**Where do I submit challenges?**
> Challenges are submitted to the dashboard at <https://ghw.mlh.io/challenges>

**Where can I find recordings of the streams?**
> You can find the records on our [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/mlh) or
> [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Majorleaguehacking) page.

**Who organizes Global Hack Week?**
> Global Hack Week is organized by [Major League Hacking (MLH)](http://mlh.io). It is our
> mission to empower hackers worldwide by providing them with the tools, resources, and
> opportunities they need to succeed.

**What happened to Local Hack Day?**
> In early 2022, we decided to change Local Hack Day to Global Hack Week. As Local Hack
> Day grew, it became a week-long event featuring technical live streams, workshops,
> career chats, panels, games, and more. We wanted to update our language to reflect how
> this event series has brought together hackers from every corner of the globe for weeks
> at a time.

### Participants

**Who can attend Global Hack Week?**
> All are welcome to attend and participate in Global Hack Week no matter your experience
> level and student status. Hundreds of hackers who have never coded before will attend
> GHW. This event is geared for folks learning to code as well as experts! Minors 13 and
> over are welcome to attend with a parent's permission.

**Is there a code of conduct?**
> We want to make sure that all MLH events are an inclusive and safe space for all
> attendees, organizers, and sponsors. For this reason, the
> [MLH Code of Conduct](https://mlh.io/code-of-conduct) should be followed throughout the
> event.

**How much does it cost to participate?**
> It is completely free for you to participate in MLH's Global Hack Week. All you need is
> a computer to join in on all the fun!

### Swag

**What's the estimated shipping timeline for letters?**
> Inside the US, you should get your letters 1-2 weeks after they send. This can mean up
> to 4-6 weeks after the event. Outside the US, you should get your letters 3-4 weeks
> after they send. This can mean up to 6-8 weeks after the event. In India particularly,
> we see wildly different delivery times in different parts of India. If it's been more
> than 12 weeks since the event, your letter may have been lost in the Indian postal
> system.

**How do I get my Swag?**
> You're receiving a letter via post. Your letter is shipped from the United States to
> your location. If you're in the US, you'll receive it from USPS. If you're outside the
> US, you'll receive it through your country's postal system.
>
> You earn swag by fully checking in to GHW. This includes joining the MLH Community
> Discord, checking in on the event registration page, and submitting an address at
> mlh.link/Address. To earn our bonus stickers, you just need to attend 5 live sessions
> throughout the week!

**Do you ship swag to India?**
> We do! Please make sure your address is updated correctly on
> [mlh.link/Address](https://hackp.ac/address).

**How much does it cost?**
> It is completely free for you to participate in MLH's Global Hack Week. All you need is
> a computer to join in on all the fun!

### Guilds

**What if I don't feel ready to organize a Guild?**
> Guilds are a fantastic way to market your hackathon, form a stronger community at your
> school, meet people with similar interests as you, or practice your leadership skills
> in a low-stakes environment.

**Why should I organize?**
> We know that there is a ton of imposter syndrome in our community. We are confident in
> each and every one of you and will make sure you have all the tools you need to shine
> as guild leader.

**What kind of guild should I form?**
> A guild can be formed around anything. Former guilds have been hosted around individual
> hackathons (shoutout to the Girls Hoo Hack guild), your timezone (shoutout to
> ESTechies), or your love of stuffed sharks from IKEA (shoutout to Blahajgang)!
