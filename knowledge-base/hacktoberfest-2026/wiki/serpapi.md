## Definition

SerpApi returns structured search results with CAPTCHA solving and location targeting.

## Key Points

- APIs [docs]: Google (Search, Maps, Shopping, News, Images, Scholar, Jobs, Flights, Hotels, AI Mode and more) and Amazon, Bing, DuckDuckGo, eBay, Walmart, Yelp, YouTube, Zillow and others.
- Free 250 searches per month, 50 per hour; paid from $25 per month; ZeroTrace no-retention mode; SOC 2 Type II [docs]. SDKs and MCP support not stated [unverified].
- Fits: grounding a local model with current facts; online by nature.
- First-hand (Week 1): used for voice-requested web search (a note that starts with an explicit phrase triggers one search); free tier, capped at 8 a day in the project's own code, key kept on the Mac. A leading filler such as "the web about" in the query returned off-topic results until it was stripped.
