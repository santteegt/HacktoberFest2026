## Definition

Global Hack Week (GHW) is a free, online, week-long MLH event held monthly. Daily livestreams, Discord mini-events, and challenges that earn experience points fill the week.

## Key Points

- **GHW: Hacktoberfest** runs Oct 9-15, 2026 (opening ceremony 10:00 AM EDT, closing 12:00 PM EDT Oct 15), themed "a week of all things open weight models". Page: https://ghw.mlh.com/events/open-source; registration: https://events.mlh.com/events/14553-global-hack-week-open-source. Do not confuse it with December's Open Source Week.
- Worth up to **5 stickers**: registration challenges, one GHW livestream check-in, and 15, 30, and 75 points ([Sticker milestones](sticker-milestones.md)).
- Challenges are submitted at https://ghw.mlh.io/challenges (redirects to the current dashboard). Per-challenge point values are not published.
- Basic swag needs full check-in: join the MLH Discord (https://discord.mlh.io), submit an address (mlh.link/Address), complete all registration challenges. Bonus stickers need 5 live sessions.
- Joining the MLH Community Discord is also a Tools sticker.
- Open question: whether a public leaderboard exists.
