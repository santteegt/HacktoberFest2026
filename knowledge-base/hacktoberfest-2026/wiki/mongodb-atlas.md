## Definition

Atlas stores documents and embeddings together and offers vector and hybrid search for RAG.

## Key Points

- HNSW approximate or exact nearest-neighbour search, pre-filtering, up to 8192 dimensions, hybrid with full-text, automated embeddings with Voyage AI or bring your own [docs].
- Works on Atlas, self-managed and local Atlas deployments via the Atlas CLI (exact command unconfirmed); M0 free-tier vector limits unverified.
- Fits: flexible-schema data with semantic search; the free tier is hosted so data leaves the device unless run locally.
