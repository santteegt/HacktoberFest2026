## Definition

The Hacktoberfest Weekend Challenge (DEV event 78) runs Oct 2 02:00 to Oct 5 06:59 UTC. The theme is to ship something with open-source AI at its core that solves a real problem for a friend or someone you love.

## Key Points

- Tag: `#hf26challenge`. Prize pool: $2,450 across 17 winners (overall $250 + DEV++, 6 featured categories at $200, 10 categories at $100). Winners: TBD.
- Judging, in order of weight: Writing Quality (most), Relevance to Prompt and Theme, Creativity, Technical Execution, optional Use of Partner Technology.
- "Pick one real person." Bonus points for handing it over and reporting what they said.
- Examples from the page: a meal planner for a roommate's allergies, a patient language practice partner, voice memos turned into a family recipe book.
- Categories and partners: [Prize categories and partners](prize-categories-and-partners.md). The prompt: [Open-source AI prompt](open-source-ai-prompt.md).
- Local project folder: `challenges/01-weekend-build-for-a-friend/`.
