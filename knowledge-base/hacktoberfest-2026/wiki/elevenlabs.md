## Definition

ElevenLabs offers low-latency TTS, Scribe speech-to-text and ElevenAgents for voice agents, with Python and TypeScript SDKs.

## Key Points

- TTS models [docs]: Eleven v4, v4 Turbo (about 100 ms), Flash v2.5 (about 75 ms, 32 languages), Multilingual v2; 10,000+ voices and cloning. Scribe v2 (90+ languages, diarisation up to 32 speakers), realtime (about 150 ms) and medical variants.
- Agents accept a custom OpenAI-compatible LLM [report]; free 10k credits per month, no commercial use [report]; promo available.
- Conflicts with an offline claim: make it an opt-in toggle. First-hand: browser on-device speech recognition handled push-to-talk without it (desktop Chrome plus a one-time speech pack).
- Fits: voice-first helpers, read-aloud and storytelling tools.
