## Definition

The second weekly Open-Source AI challenge (DEV event 80, tags `devchallenge` and `#hf26challenge`, Oct 12 19:00 to Oct 19 06:59 UTC). The theme asks for something built on open-weight models or open-source AI that someone would actually pay for, a post that says who pays and why open innovation matters, and offers bonus points for a first user. It added a $100 category for a .tech domain.

## Key Points

- Entry: PartsPaddock, a WhatsApp parts desk agent for RC clubs. P2P (free): HAVE and NEED listings matched inside a club and across clubs in the same city, with private intros. B2C (paid): import quotes from a US hobby shop (store price + weight × per-pound fee, +12% if the maintainer orders it), a deposit receipt PDF confirmed by the maintainer or a self-order tracking number, and an order batch.
- Stack: [Hermes Agent](hermes-agent.md) profile on the Baileys WhatsApp bridge, [Gemma](gemma.md) 4 26B A4B via [OpenRouter](openrouter.md), a Python CLI, Postgres on [Tiger Data](tiger-data.md). [Cognee](cognee.md) evaluated as the open memory layer and kept as backlog.
- Categories entered: Gemma and Tiger Data.
- First user: a fellow club racer used it from their own phone (quote, test deposit PDF, confirmation); the post reports their reaction and no payment.
- Published 2026-10-18 23:21 local (about 2 h 38 min before the deadline): https://dev.to/santteegt/partspaddock-a-whatsapp-parts-desk-agent-for-rc-clubs-p2p-b2c-market-connecting-hobbyists-14oa
- Outcome unknown at the time of writing; winners are announced the week of Oct 19.
