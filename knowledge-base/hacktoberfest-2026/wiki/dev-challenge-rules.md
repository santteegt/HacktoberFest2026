## Definition

DEV Challenges are online mini-hackathons entered by publishing a DEV post with the challenge's required tag. DEV's Official Challenges and Hackathon Rules (sponsored by MLH) govern them; each challenge page adds specifics, and the Official Rules win if they conflict.

## Key Points

- **Eligibility:** 18+, an active DEV account, not in an excluded jurisdiction (e.g. Russia, Iran, Cuba, Venezuela; full list in the source), and not a sponsor employee or household member.
- **Entry:** use the submission template with the required tag (Hacktoberfest: `#hf26challenge`); show what you built, why, and a demo; include test credentials if a login is needed.
- **Teams:** up to four; one submission per team; list teammates' DEV handles; DEV does not split prizes.
- **Submissions:** Weekend Challenges allow one per person. Multi-path challenges allow one per path. One win per participant per challenge.
- **New work only:** entry must be started in the window; later commits must be noted in the README.
- **Credit prior work.** Plagiarism means loss of prize eligibility or disqualification, and egregious cases mean suspension from DEV.
- **AI use is allowed**, and DEV's AI guidelines and `ai_disclosure_level` still apply to the post.
- **Language:** non-English entries get a completion badge but are not prize-eligible.
- **Judging:** criteria are per prompt; ties go to the entry with more positive reactions on its post.
- **Payout:** DEV emails winners within 10 business days; paperwork (affidavit, tax info) within 7 business days or the prize is forfeited. Winners pay taxes.
- **Monthly MLH x DEV Writing Challenge** (`#mlhacks`, $100 per selected project) is a separate standing program.
