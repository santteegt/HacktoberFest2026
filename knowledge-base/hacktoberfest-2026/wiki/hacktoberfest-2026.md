## Definition

Hacktoberfest 2026 is a free, month-long celebration of open source stewarded by MLH and DEV (presented by DigitalOcean) with the theme "AI belongs to everyone": building with open-weight models and open-source AI.

## Key Points

- **Pull requests no longer count.** Models and articles describing the old "open 4 (or 6) PRs" rules are wrong for 2026. The `hacktoberfest` repo topic and `hacktoberfest-accepted` label earn nothing.
- Participants sign in with MyMLH and collect **virtual stickers**; see [Sticker milestones](sticker-milestones.md).
- Online activities: five DEV Challenges ([weekend](weekend-challenge-build-for-a-friend.md) plus four weekly rounds), MLH livestreams, and [Global Hack Week](global-hack-week.md). In person: [Fests](fests.md).
- Participants must be 13+; DEV Challenges have their own 18+ rules ([DEV Challenge rules](dev-challenge-rules.md)).
- The final activity list lives on the dashboard, https://hacktoberfest.com/my/. The site also publishes `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` for agents.
- Dates: see [Hacktoberfest 2026 schedule](hacktoberfest-2026-schedule.md).
