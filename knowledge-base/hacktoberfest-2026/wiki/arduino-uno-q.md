## Definition

The UNO Q pairs a Qualcomm QRB2210 quad Cortex-A53 running Debian with an STM32U585 microcontroller, bridged by an RPC library.

## Key Points

- Specs [docs]: Cortex-A53 at 2.0 GHz with Adreno GPU; Cortex-M33 at 160 MHz, 2 MB flash, 786 KB SRAM; Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.1; Qwiic, UNO headers, MIPI-CSI/DSI.
- Arduino App Lab composes apps from reusable Bricks; Edge Impulse integration for custom models [report]; AI brick details unverified.
- Effort is high for a three-day window and the board must be in hand.
- Fits: a physical sensor-driven helper (camera, microphone, motion) with on-device inference.
