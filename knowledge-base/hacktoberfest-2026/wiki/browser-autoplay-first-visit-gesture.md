## Definition

Browsers refuse to start audio before a user gesture. Instead of a muted site or a bare "enable sound" button, make the first click a themed action that also starts the sound.

## Key Points

- Week 4 example: the Eth Ghosts gallery shows "Hunt the ghost" on the index and "Light the candle" on a ghost page; the first click lights the page and starts the music.
- The audio asset was re-encoded from 5.66 MB to 1.70 MB so the first click does not stall on a download.
- Checked only in headless Chromium; Safari, Firefox, a real phone and a screen reader were not tested, so do not claim cross-browser behaviour.
