## Summary

DevRelay's knowledge-base guide to Hacktoberfest 2026 (last verified 2026-09-30 against hacktoberfest.com and its public feeds). It explains that pull requests no longer count and that participants earn virtual stickers instead, lists all 24 stickers, the 3/10/15 milestones, key dates, the five DEV Challenge rounds, GHW: Hacktoberfest, and how Fests are found and hosted. It flags that the Completionist tier already dropped from 17 to 15 stickers and that the dashboard is the final authority.

## Notable Points

- Rewards come from stickers; the repo topic and `hacktoberfest-accepted` label earn nothing.
- Two shipping timelines are published (8-12 weeks vs 30-60 days); the guide asks readers to flag the difference.
- The guide's "does not know" list includes how stickers are verified (including the DevRelay one) and how the Completionist raffle is drawn.
