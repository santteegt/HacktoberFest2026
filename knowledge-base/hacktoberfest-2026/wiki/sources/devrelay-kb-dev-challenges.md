## Summary

DevRelay's guide to DEV's Official Challenges and Hackathon Rules (verified 2026-09-30). It covers who can enter (18+, DEV Members, excluded jurisdictions), how to enter by publishing a DEV post with the challenge's required tag, rules that repeat across challenge FAQs (teams, submission counts, new work only, plagiarism, AI use, language), judging and tie-breaks, and how winners are paid. It also describes the standing Monthly MLH x DEV Writing Challenge (`#mlhacks`, $100 per selected project).

## Notable Points

- Each challenge page overrides the general patterns, and the Official Rules override the challenge page.
- Ties are broken by positive reactions on the DEV post.
- Non-English submissions get a completion badge but are not prize-eligible.
