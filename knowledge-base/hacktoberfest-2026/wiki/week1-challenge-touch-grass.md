## Definition

The first of the four weekly Open-Source AI challenges (DEV event 79, tag `#hf26challenge`, Oct 5 18:00 to Oct 12 06:59 UTC). The prompt asks for open-source AI at the core of something that makes the screen the shortest part of an outdoor experience, and for a post that says why open innovation mattered for the build.

## Key Points

- Entry: Idea Leash, an open-source walk buddy. A podcast link sent to a Telegram bot becomes verified notes and a five-question quiz in an OKF wiki; a phone web app records the walk offline; the Mac sends an after-walk summary and grades the quiz in Telegram; badges and distances are plain code.
- Open stack on one M4 Max Mac: [Gemma](gemma.md) 4 12B QAT through [Ollama](ollama.md), [Hermes Agent](hermes-agent.md), whisper.cpp, graphify, Piper, Lume. Not open: Telegram, Tailscale, [SerpApi](serpapi.md), the podcast host, macOS in the VM.
- Categories entered: Gemma and SerpApi, only where the code really used them.
- Published 2026-10-11 23:30 local (about 2.5 h before the deadline): https://dev.to/santteegt/idea-leash-an-open-source-walk-buddy-with-a-local-model-for-a-brain-499n
- Outcome unknown at the time of writing; winners are announced the week of Oct 12.
