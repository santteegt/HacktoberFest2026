## Definition

Hacktoberfest 2026 rewards virtual stickers earned from sign-in, livestreams, DEV Challenges, Global Hack Week, tools, surveys, and in-person Fests.

## Key Points

- **3 stickers:** a mailed sticker pack. **10:** a bonus holographic sticker. **15 (Completionist):** raffle entry for a t-shirt or an Arduino Uno Q.
- Two **required** stickers come first: sign in and add a mailing address. Signing in alone does not qualify.
- 24 stickers exist: Required 2, Livestreams 4, DEV Challenges 6, Global Hack Week 5, Tools 3, Surveys 2, In person 2. The 22 non-in-person stickers mean the 15 tier is reachable fully online.
- The Completionist tier dropped from 17 to 15 during Preptember; confirm on the dashboard before quoting thresholds.
- Online participants are not promised a t-shirt; it comes only via the raffle.
- Shipping timelines conflict on the site (8-12 weeks vs 30-60 days).
- The repo-level tracker is `STICKERS.md` at the repository root.
