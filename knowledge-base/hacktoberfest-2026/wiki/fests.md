## Definition

A Fest is a free, one-day, in-person Hacktoberfest event about open-source AI. There are 300+ worldwide.

## Key Points

- **Hack Day:** a mini hackathon with prizes, MLH-funded. **Meetup:** talks, workshops, or a panel, no project and no MLH funding.
- Each Fest has its own registration (free MyMLH account) and check-in on the day.
- Hack Day teams may be any size, but prizes cover up to four members; every member must register and check in.
- Attending or hosting earns a sticker (and a DEV badge); attendees get a participation certificate.
- Find Fests via `https://hacktoberfest-api.mlh.com/api/events` (no auth) and confirm the date on the Fest's page; some listings show `status: pending`.
- Host applications: https://hacktoberfest.com/host/, confirmed within about a week.
