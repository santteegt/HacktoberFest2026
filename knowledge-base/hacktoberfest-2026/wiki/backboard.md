## Definition

Backboard is a hosted AI infrastructure with persistent hybrid memory, built-in RAG and routing across providers, with Python and JS/TS SDKs.

## Key Points

- Memory extracts facts automatically with `memory="Auto"` and shares them across threads at the assistant level; mid-conversation model switching [docs].
- $5 free credits for 30 days; routes to open models via other providers [report]; data leaves the device.
- Fits: companions and tutors that need long-term memory about one person.
