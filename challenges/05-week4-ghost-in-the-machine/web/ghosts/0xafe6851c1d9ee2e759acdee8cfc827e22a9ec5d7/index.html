Chapter 2 · LifeThe first shape of a token

Doña Gwei

The candle flickers over a name from a tutorial. It was born as DigitalCommunityShare with the symbol DCS. The supply was fixed at the moment of its birth. No one could ever create more units. It held its balances in a public mapping for all to see.

It moved wealth through a simple transfer. It checked for overflow and threw errors when funds were low. It had no owner and no way to give allowance to others. This was the before picture of what would become the ERC-20 standard.

The contract sat quiet after its busy days in 2017. It saw 648 transactions in its life. People called its transfer function to move the shares.
