# Lane C3: gallery v2 (2026-10-29, M4 Max, Claude Opus 5.5)

The author's six review points on the live gallery (25 ghosts), all implemented. Not deployed, not committed.

## What changed
1. **Papel picado strip**: `web/index.html` inlines `template/characters/papel-picado.svg` verbatim (`$uid` = `index`), with the story pages' `.picado` and `.topbar` rules. The old CSS triangle bunting is gone. The palette in `site.css` now uses the template's values (night `#1b0b2b`, candle `#ffd24a`, rosa `#e8457a`, lilac, teal), the dotted night background, the rounded title font with the ink and rosa shadow, and the story pages' kind badge colors (white on dark, contrast 6.4:1 or better, per Lane C1's numbers).
2. **Name**: the document title and the kicker are exactly "Ghosts in the Decentralized World Computer". The H1 stays "Ethereum Ghosts".
3. **Story of the day**: `ghosts/gallery.py` also writes `web/ghosts/featured.json`, which holds the newest entry (same keys as an index entry, `cover` included, plus `generated_at`), or `null` when there are no ghosts. `index.json` is still a plain array. `site.js` fetches both and falls back to `index[0]` when `featured.json` is missing or invalid. The card sits between the intro and the filters. It shows the night stage (stars, moon), the sheet ghost cloned with the ghost's kind face and its address tag, and the cover in a tilted frame when there is one. Beside the stage it shows the kicker, title, name and short address, tagline, chips (kind, age, scarred, never called, proxy, historian) and four tiles (born, last seen or "never", calls, balance). The "Learn more" link has screen-reader text naming the ghost.
4. **Haunting ghost**: a fixed, `pointer-events:none` layer holds the friendly sheet ghost (tag `0xB00`). It drifts over the whole viewport on a 90 s path and bobs. The "Lights" toggle: when on, the ghost shows only as a faint silhouette (`brightness(0) invert(.88)` at 13 % opacity). When off, a dark overlay dims the page and the ghost glows at full color. The toggles and the legend stay above the overlay. "Ghost flies" pauses or resumes both animations. Both are `<button aria-pressed>`, persisted as `ghosts.lights` and `ghosts.motion` in localStorage with try/catch around every read and write. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the flight is off unless the visitor turns it on, and an `!important` override then honors that choice. Without JS the ghost stays still.
5. **Floating legend**: an `<aside>` docked on the right with a "Key" button and seven stacked badges (the five kinds, scarred, never called). On hover or `:focus-within` (only on hover-capable devices) and on tapping Key (`aria-expanded`, Escape and outside-click close it), it widens to show one-line descriptions. The descriptions are always in the DOM, so screen readers get them. Below 700 px it docks at the bottom as one scrolling row and opens upward into a list. At 700 px and up, `.wrap` gets right padding so the rail never covers content. The filter chips are unchanged. Cards now also show a "never called" tag.
6. **About**: the closed-model template sentence is removed. Everything else is kept.

- The lights-on silhouette is light at low opacity, not a dark fill: a dark shape cannot be seen on the night background.
- When paused, the ghost stops where it is (play-state paused); it does not go back to a resting spot.
- Not checked in Safari or Firefox, or on a real phone. Playwright used Chromium with touch emulation at 375 px.
- The featured ghost shows the kind's face; banished ghosts get the faded X-eyes ghost, not the gravestone.
