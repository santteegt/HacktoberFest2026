Idea Leash walk test

Checks what a web page can do on a real walk: GPS, screen kept on while black, voice clips saved offline, a beep, and whether your podcast app keeps playing.

  1. Open YouTube Premium (or your podcast app) and start an episode. Then come back to this page.
  2. Tap Start walk and allow location and microphone.
  3. Screen goes black. Put the phone in your pocket. Walk about 20 minutes.
  4. Tap the big Record button in the middle to start a voice idea, tap again to stop. A beep plays on the interval below.
  5. To finish, hold the boxed corner at the top left for 3 seconds. Then read the report and copy it.
